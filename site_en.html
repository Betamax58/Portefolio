PRESENTATION

Hello!
My name is Kévin and I will soon graduate from the École Supérieure d'Ingénieurs en Génie Électrique (ESIGELEC) in Rouen, with a major in Medical Systems Engineering. Passionate about innovative technologies and their applications in the healthcare sector, I strive to develop solutions that combine performance with a positive impact.

Alongside my studies, I serve as the president of the electronics association at ESIGELEC. This role allows me to coordinate technical projects, mentor a team of passionate students, and promote the sharing of knowledge in electronics within the school. This experience has given me the opportunity to acquire skills in project management, leadership, and technical collaboration.

-- Texte caché -- As part of my final year of studies, I am currently seeking a technical internship in France or abroad, available immediately. If you are interested, feel free to check out my projects or my CV! -- Texte caché --
