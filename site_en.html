Hello!
My name is Kévin and I am currently in my final year at ESIGELEC in Rouen, majoring in Medical Systems Engineering. I am currently seeking an engineering position and am passionate about innovative technologies and their applications in the healthcare sector, I strive to develop solutions that combine performance with a positive impact.
